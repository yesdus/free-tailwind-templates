Ask something else
Is my money protected?

Yes. Farthing is a licensed electronic money institution, and every euro of your balance is held in a safeguarding account at a central bank, apart from our own money.

Can I keep my current bank as well?

Many people do for the first months. You can send payouts to Farthing and move the rest when you are ready; we switch your direct debits for you on the House plan.

What if I change the tax percentage?

It applies from the next sale. Money already in the pot stays there until the filing date, unless you ask the night desk to release it.

Do I need a card reader?

Not to start. Your phone takes contactless payments from the first evening. Countertop readers come with the Counter and House plans.

How do I leave?

Close the account from the app. Your balance and pots are paid out the same day and your statements stay downloadable for seven years.
