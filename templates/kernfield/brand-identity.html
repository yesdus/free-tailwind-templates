Brand identity

Length
12 weeks from 7 April
Group
12 students at most
Level
Beginner friendly

What happens each week

You work on one real brief for a local organisation the whole term. Every session mixes a short talk, studio time and a group crit.

Weeks 1–2 Listening

Interview the client, map their audience and write a one-page brand strategy in plain language.

Weeks 3–5 Marks & words

Sketch fifty marks, narrow to three, and test them against a naming and tone-of-voice exercise.

Weeks 6–8 The system

Type, colour, grid and imagery rules that let someone else use the brand without you.

Weeks 9–10 In the world

Signage, social templates, packaging or uniforms: whatever your client actually needs.

Weeks 11–12 Guidelines & crit

Bind a short guideline book and present it to the client and a visiting creative director.
