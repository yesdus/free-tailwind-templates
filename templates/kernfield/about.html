Since 2015

We started with one room and a borrowed projector

Three designers wanted to teach the things they learned on the job, at hours that suited people with jobs. Twelve students came to the first class.

Eleven years later we have four studios in an old foundry, forty tutors who still practise, and a bursary fund that pays for one seat in three.

How we teach

01

Make every week

No lectures without a pencil. Each session ends with something on the wall.

02

Crit kindly, crit honestly

Feedback is specific, about the work, and given by people who hire designers.

03

Keep groups small

Twelve at most, so every student gets real time with their tutor.

04

Price for people

Payment plans and bursaries on every course, no questions about why.

Eleven years, briefly

  1. 2015

    First class: twelve people, one borrowed projector.

  2. 2018

    Move to Foundry Yard and open two studios.

  3. 2021

    Online courses launch, students join from 40 countries.

  4. 2025

    Bursary fund passes one thousand funded places.
