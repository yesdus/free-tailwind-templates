Features

Built Around How You Feel

Four quiet systems work together so every session matches the body you woke up with.

Daily check-in

Thirty seconds each morning. Tell Oruna how you slept, how sore you are and how much time you have, and it rewrites today on the spot.

  • Voice or text, your choice
  • Reads sleep and HRV from your wearable
  • Remembers what you said last week

Adaptive plans

Plans shift with your week. Miss a day and Oruna reshuffles; crush a workout and it nudges the next one up a notch.

  • Rebalances load across the week
  • Swaps sessions when you travel
  • Never stacks two hard days

Run coach

Live pace, heart rate and route cues in your ear, with a replay of every run so you can see exactly where you pushed.

  • Audio cues for pace and zones
  • Route replay with splits
  • Automatic personal bests

Sleep & recovery

Recovery scores built from sleep, strain and stress, plus breathwork and mobility when your body asks for a slower day.

  • Nightly recovery score
  • Guided breathwork sessions
  • Mobility flows under 15 minutes
