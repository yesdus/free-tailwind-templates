The Science Inside

Oruna turns a handful of quiet signals into one clear answer each morning: how hard should you go today?

Heart rate variability

Measured overnight, HRV shows how recovered your nervous system is. A low reading means Oruna softens today's plan.

Tap the points on the band to explore each signal.

How Oruna Learns You

  1. 1

    Week 1

    Baseline

    Oruna watches quietly. It learns your resting heart rate, sleep window and how you respond to your usual sessions.

  2. 2

    Week 2

    Patterns

    It starts to spot links, like how late dinners shorten your deep sleep or how Monday runs feel harder after busy weekends.

  3. 3

    Week 4

    Your model

    A personal coaching model takes over. Plans now shift daily, and check-ins get shorter because Oruna already knows the answer.

  4. 4

    Ongoing

    Always learning

    Seasons, travel, injuries and new goals all feed back in. The plan grows with you instead of resetting every twelve weeks.

Your data stays yours

  • On-device first

    Most of the model runs on your phone. Raw heart data never has to leave it.

  • Encrypted sync

    Backups are encrypted end to end, and you can delete everything in one tap.

  • Never sold

    No ads and no data brokers. Your subscription is our only business model.
