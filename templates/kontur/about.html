Noa Lindqvist

I started in motion graphics, cutting title sequences for documentaries, and drifted into interaction design when the screens got smaller. Today I work as an independent art director with a small circle of developers, photographers and writers.

I like projects with one strong idea and the patience to see it through every format, from a favicon to a billboard.
