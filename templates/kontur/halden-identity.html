Role
Art direction, identity

The brief

Halden roasts in batches of twelve kilos and sells most of it to the cafés within cycling distance. They wanted to sell online without looking like every other speciality roaster: no kraft paper, no hand-drawn mountains.

The idea became heat. Each roast level gets its own gradient, sampled from photos of the beans at the moment they leave the drum, and the wordmark is cut so it can sit across a fold in the bag.

The result

    online orders in the first quarter

  • 14

    cafés now stocking the bags

  • 1

    gradient per roast, no exceptions
