Oblique began as a paper pattern on a rented table. We still start every garment the same way: one shape, one fibre, one atelier that already knows the piece by name.

The studio

A quieter way to dress

We design in a loft above a courtyard in Lisbon and sew almost nothing under our own roof. Pattern, fit, and fabric decisions stay here. Construction travels to the same workshops we have used since the first drop.

The result is a short line: outerwear, knits, a few dresses, and the leather pieces that finish them. Nothing seasonal for the sake of it. Nothing you have to explain.

Our ateliers

Named rooms, not factories

We visit each workshop twice a year. The people who sew our clothes know the pattern numbers by heart, and we publish the city on every product page.

Portugal

Porto

Tailoring and structured outerwear

A three-generation family workshop that still cuts wool on the table, not on a conveyor. Every Oblique blazer and coat starts here.

Italy

Prato

Brushed wool and cashmere coats

A mill-adjacent atelier that finishes our camel and ink overcoats — bound seams, corozo buttons, and a press that takes the afternoon.

Poland

Łódź

Washed cupro and light wovens

A small cutting room that handles our summer sets. They wash, drape, and sew the playsuits and shirts that need a softer hand.
