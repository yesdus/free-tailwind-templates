Why we keep them

Fashion moves fast and throws most of its pictures away. We started Velvoro in a Milan basement with three boxes of negatives a friend was about to bin. Today we work with thirty-eight photographers to scan, credit and publish the frames that never ran.

Every story is printed small, edited slowly and paid for. Photographers keep their rights, always.
