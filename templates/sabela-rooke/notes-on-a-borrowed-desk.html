A Desk

Notes on a Borrowed Desk

Notebook · 4 minutes

Notebook

Three weeks in a friend's flat, at a desk that still holds another person's pencils. A few things I wrote down.

The desk is not mine, which is the best thing about it. None of the unfinished things in its drawers are my fault.

Her pencils are all sharpened to the same cruel point. I have not dared to use one. I write with the biro I found in my coat, like a guest who brought her own towel.

The window faces a wall. I thought I would mind. It turns out a wall asks nothing of you, and a view is a kind of colleague.

“A wall asks nothing of you, and a view is a kind of colleague.”

Things written at this desk so far: one essay, forty postcards, a list of every kitchen I have lived in. Things written at my own desk last month: invoices.

I go home on Sunday. I am considering turning my desk to face the wall.
