Membership questions

Can I cancel anytime?

Monthly plans cancel with one click. Yearly plans can be refunded pro rata within the first 60 days.

Does membership replace insurance?

No. Membership covers primary and preventive care at Clinova. Keep insurance for hospital and specialist costs.

Are lab tests included?

Complete and Family include quarterly panels. Extra tests are billed at member rates, usually 30% lower.
