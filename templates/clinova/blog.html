What your yearly check-up really tells you

A check-up is not a pass or fail test. It is a snapshot that becomes useful when compared year to year.

Ask for your numbers, not just a thumbs up. Blood pressure, fasting glucose and cholesterol trends tell you far more than any single reading.

Hydration myths we hear every week

Eight glasses a day is a rough guide, not a rule. Needs change with heat, exercise and what you eat.

Pale yellow urine is the simplest check. Coffee and tea count too, despite what you may have heard.
