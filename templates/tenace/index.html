Got a question?

I've never played bridge. Where do I start?

Begin with Bidding Basics. It assumes no experience and gets you playing full hands by the second week.

Do I need a partner?

No. Practice deals pair you with a computer partner, and live lessons match you with other students.

Can I learn on my phone?

Yes. Lessons and practice deals work on any phone, tablet or computer, and your progress syncs between them.

Do you offer group rates for clubs?

The Club plan covers up to 30 members. Larger clubs can ask us for a custom quote.

Which bidding system do you teach?

Standard American and Acol, so you can play comfortably at clubs on both sides of the Atlantic.
