Our Story

From One Shared
Compartment

In 2019 our founders met on the sleeper from Lisbon to Madrid, both travelling solo, both wishing they had company for the hike ahead. They finished the trail together and came home with an idea.

Roamia matches people on what matters on the road: pace, budget, and the kind of days that make a trip worth telling.
