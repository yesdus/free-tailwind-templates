Privacy

Privacy Policy

1

What we collect

Your name, email, travel preferences and the identity document you use to verify your profile. We only ask for what matching and safety need.

2

How we use it

To suggest compatible explorers, keep the community safe and send the updates you asked for. We never sell your data.

3

Who can see it

Other members see your first name, photo and travel style. Your documents and contact details stay private until you choose to share them.

4

Your choices

Download or delete your data at any time from your account, or write to privacy@roamia.example and we will do it for you.
