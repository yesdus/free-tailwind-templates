Help Center

Answers Before
You Ask

How does matching work?

Tell us your pace, budget and the kind of trips you love. We suggest explorers heading the same way, and you decide who to talk to.

Is every member verified?

Yes. Each profile passes an identity check before it can send a message, and travelers review each other after every trip.

Can I travel with a group?

You can match one to one or open a trip to a small group of up to eight people, with a shared itinerary and budget.

What if my plans change?

Update or cancel a trip from your itinerary at any time. Your matches are told straight away so nobody is left waiting.
