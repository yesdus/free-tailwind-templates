Questions we hear first

How long is a typical engagement?

Eight to twelve weeks for most diagnostic and design work, followed by an optional quarter of implementation support.

Do you implement or only advise?

Both. We stay with your owners through the first quarter of change, and we measure ourselves on the numbers, not the deck.

How do you price?

A fixed fee per phase, agreed before we start. No hourly billing and no surprise extensions.

Do you work on success fees?

For cost and cash programmes we can tie part of the fee to verified savings, capped and agreed in writing.

Who will actually do the work?

The partner you meet leads the work every week. Teams are two to five people, and there are no junior handovers.

Can you work alongside our own strategy team?

Yes, and we prefer it. Half of every engagement is leaving your people able to do it without us.
