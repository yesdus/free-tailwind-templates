The problem

The best local experiences are the hardest ones to book

Most hosts run on phone calls, spreadsheets and goodwill. Global platforms can't plug into them, so travelers end up with the same handful of generic tours.

  • Fragmented inventory across thousands of tiny operators
  • No shared format for stories, photos or availability
  • Payouts that take weeks and eat into thin margins
