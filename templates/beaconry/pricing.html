talk to a person

asked before signing

what counts as a device?
Anything that reported at least once in the month. A sensor in a drawer costs nothing.
do you sell the hardware?
No. Bring what you have. We keep a list of makers we have tested and are happy to say which ones behave.
where does the data live?
In Frankfurt and Amsterdam, on servers we rent, encrypted. It never leaves the EU unless you export it.
can we leave?
Yes. Export everything, cancel the same day, and we delete it thirty days later. No notice period.
is there a contract?
Monthly by default. Yearly if you want the two months off and an invoice your finance team prefers.
what about 50,000 devices?
Then the slider on this site is too short. Write to us and we will make a price that fits.
