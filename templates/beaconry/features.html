features

four jobs,
done properly, instead of forty done halfway.

Built with the people who carry the on-call phone for 14,200 fridges, meters, bikes and roof sensors.

01

see every device

One map, one list, one search box. Bring devices from any maker and they show the same four things: where it is, when it last spoke, how much battery is left, and whether anything is wrong.

  • Works with 60+ device makers
  • Groups by site, customer or floor
  • Search by serial, name or note

02

hear about it first

Write a rule the way you would say it out loud. Beaconry watches every reading against it and tells one person, in the place they already look, with the device and the reason.

  • Rules in plain words
  • Quiet hours and on-call rotas
  • Slack, Teams, SMS, PagerDuty

03

update without a van

Send new firmware to ten devices first. If they come back healthy, the next wave goes by itself. If two fail, everything stops and the ten go back to what they had.

  • Waves you set once
  • Automatic rollback
  • A log of who sent what, when

04

keep the receipts

Every reading, alert and change is stored for seven years in the EU. Export a month for one site in two clicks when the inspector, the insurer or the customer asks.

  • Seven years, EU storage
  • CSV, Snowflake, BigQuery
  • Signed, tamper-evident exports
