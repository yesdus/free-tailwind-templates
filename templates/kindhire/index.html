How it works

From hello to hired in about two months

More about the platform

  1. Step 1

    Sign up in your own way

    Online, by phone, by text or over a sign language video call.

  2. Step 2

    Free skills check

    A relaxed chat with a career coach about what you enjoy and what you need.

  3. Step 3

    Paid training

    Six weeks of online training, paid from day one, at your own pace.

  4. Step 4

    Your first role

    We introduce you to an employer who has already set up your accommodations.

  5. Step 5

    Ongoing support

    A coach checks in every month for your first year, and more if you want.
