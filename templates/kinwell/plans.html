Good to know

Straight answers before you sign up.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Monthly plans cancel with one tap in the app. Yearly plans can be refunded pro rata in the first 60 days.

Is medication included?

Most generic medications are included. Branded options are shown with a clear price before you confirm.

Do I need insurance?

No. Kinwell plans are self-pay with transparent pricing. We can provide receipts for reimbursement.
