[ About us ]

Calm money advice, for everyone.

We started Fernwise because good financial guidance shouldn't depend on how much you already have.

[ Our story ]

Ten years of growing, slowly and on purpose.

  1. 2016

    Two advisers, one kitchen table

    Nadia and Theo leave a private bank to offer fee-only advice to teachers and nurses.

  2. 2018

    The first app

    A simple budgeting app reaches 10,000 members, almost entirely by word of mouth.

  3. 2021

    Licensed to invest

    Fernwise becomes a registered investment adviser and launches managed portfolios.

  4. 2023

    56 currencies

    Multi-currency accounts open Fernwise to families and freelancers across borders.

  5. 2026

    120,000 members

    Members now hold $2.4B with us, and 95% say they feel clearer about money.
