Good to know

Do you take walk-ins?
Yes, when a chair is free. Booking guarantees your spot, especially on Saturdays.
How early should I arrive?
Five minutes is perfect. If you're more than 15 minutes late we may need to shorten the service.
Do you cut long hair?

Can I buy gift cards?
