Questions

Can I pause my plan?
Yes, up to six weeks a year with no fee.
Is there a contract?
Monthly plans roll monthly. Annual plans save 15% and can be transferred.
Do you offer corporate plans?
Yes. Teams of five or more get pooled sessions and a shared dashboard.
I'm a complete beginner. Is that okay?
Absolutely. Your first two weeks are an assessment phase built for exactly that.
How long are sessions?
HIIT runs 30 minutes, strength and personal training 50 to 60.
Do I need a heart rate monitor?
We lend chest straps for every class, or you can pair your own.
What's in the recovery suite?
Sauna, cold plunge, compression boots and a stretching zone.
Do you help with nutrition?
Wellness plans include a monthly nutrition check-in with our dietitian.
Can I train with an injury?
Often, yes. Tell us first and we'll build sessions around it with our physio.
