Kaiyo Bistro was born from a vision by Chef Hiroshi Sato to bring the soul of Hokkaido’s coastal cuisine to the city. Starting as a small noodle bar, it evolved into a sanctuary for food lovers seeking authenticity without compromise.

Our commitment to seasonal ingredients and time-honored techniques ensures every visit is a journey through Japan's rich culinary landscape.

Over the decade, we have expanded our craft, blending traditional Edomae sushi styles with contemporary flavor profiles. Our kitchen is a theater of precision, where every slice and seasoning is deliberate.

Ocean to Table

We source our seafood daily from local fishermen and premium international markets to ensure the highest quality sashimi grade experience.
