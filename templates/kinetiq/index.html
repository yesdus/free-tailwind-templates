Words about our company

We are a crew of hands-on operators spread across six countries. Whether you're a two-person startup or a corporate giant that has lost its speed, we help you get moving again. Our playbook comes from years of work with ambitious founders, research labs, global firms and public bodies.

More than 1,400 founders have gone through our programmes. We've helped companies design innovation tracks that actually ship, scout new technology, train their people and launch products customers pay for.

Values we live by

We exist to get things moving. Everything we do — for founders and for corporates alike — follows these two rules.

Curiosity first

We question the obvious, try the untested and enjoy the ride while we're at it.

Honest impact

We take on work that changes something. If we can't help, we'll say so straight away.

A note from our founder

Most markets reward playing it safe. We started Kinetiq to reward the opposite: new companies that try something hard, and old ones willing to reinvent themselves. We've sat on both sides of the table, raised money, missed targets and shipped products people loved.

That's why we can help. We know what it takes to find customers, earn their trust and build something they keep using. And honestly — we just like hard problems.

— Lena Marković, founder
