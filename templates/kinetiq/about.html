Built by founders, for builders

We started as four operators who kept getting the same phone call: “Can you help us move faster?” A decade later, that's still the only question we answer.

Our story

  1. 2014

    A borrowed desk

    Four founders start coaching friends' startups from a shared office in Berlin.

  2. 2016

    First programme

    Our founder sprint takes in twelve teams. Eight of them raise money within a year.

  3. 2019

    Corporates call

    A national bank asks us to teach its teams to ship like a startup. Corporate labs are born.

  4. 2022

    Six countries

    Teams in Helsinki, Madrid, Rotterdam, Ljubljana and Belgrade join the crew.

  5. 2026

    1,400 founders

    Our alumni have raised close to a billion euros and still send us their hardest problems.
