For startups

Twelve weeks to real traction

Apply for spring cohort

How the sprint works

The first six weeks cost you nothing. If you stay for the full programme, we take a small equity stake — no fees, ever.

  1. Weeks 1–2

    Find the problem

    Customer interviews, market maps and one sharp sentence about why you exist.

    Free

  2. Weeks 3–6

    Prove it

    A scrappy MVP in front of 50 real users, with weekly coaching from operators.

    Free

  3. Weeks 7–10

    Sell it

    Pricing tests, a repeatable sales motion and your first paying customers.

    €0 + 3% equity

  4. Weeks 11–12

    Raise it

    Pitch coaching, warm intros to 60 investors and a demo day on our stage.

    Included

Before you apply

Who can apply?
Teams of two or more with a working prototype or a strong problem insight. Any sector, any European country.
Do I need to relocate?
No. We run sessions in Berlin and online. Demo day is in person, travel covered.
What if we already raised?
Pre-seed and seed teams are welcome. Join the scale track if you've raised more than €2M.
When is the deadline?
Spring cohort closes on 15 February. We reply to every application within ten days.
