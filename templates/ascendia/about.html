About

Built by
founders

We started Ascendia because the advice we found was either too polished or too vague. We wanted numbers, mistakes and the decisions behind them.

How we got here

  1. 2019

    A group chat

    Six founders share their real numbers every Sunday. It becomes the most useful hour of their week.

  2. 2021

    The first issue

    The chat turns into a newsletter for 300 friends of friends. No ads, no sponsors.

  3. 2023

    Readers fund it

    A pay-what-you-can membership lets us pay every writer and keep it ad-free.

  4. 2025

    Events begin

    Clinics online and breakfasts in six cities. Still no pitching allowed.

  5. 2026

    41,000 readers

    And still written by founders, for founders, every Sunday.
