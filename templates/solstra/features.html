The small print, in large type

Can Solstra change anything in our tools?
No. Every connector is read-only, and we ask for the narrowest scope each tool offers.
Where is our data kept?
In Frankfurt, encrypted, for 24 hours. After the brief is sent, the night's source data is deleted.
Is a model trained on our work?
Never. Your data is used to write your brief and for nothing else.
What if the brief gets something wrong?
Every sentence links to its source. Mark a line as wrong and the ranking learns from it the same night.
