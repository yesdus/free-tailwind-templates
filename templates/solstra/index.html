While you sleep

Three quiet things happen before you wake.

  1. 01 Gather
  2. 02 Weigh
  3. 03 Write

01 Gather

It reads everything, so you do not have to.

From midnight, Solstra collects what moved in your calendar, code, tickets, pipeline and inbox. Nothing is copied out of your tools, and nothing is kept longer than a day.

  • 2,400 events on a typical night
  • Read-only access, always

02 Weigh

It decides what deserves a sentence.

Most of what happens overnight is noise. Solstra ranks each change by who it blocks, how late it is and whether it is new, and drops the rest without ceremony.

  • Nine in ten events never reach you
  • You can see what was left out

03 Write

It writes like a colleague, not a dashboard.

The brief is plain prose with three headings: changed, stuck, yours. It takes two minutes to read and arrives at the time you choose, by email, in Slack or on your phone.

  • About 240 words
  • One brief per person, not per team
