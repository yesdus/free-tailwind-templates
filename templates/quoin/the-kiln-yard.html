The Kiln Yard

Place
Hunslet, Leeds
Finished
March 2025
Size
84 homes, 9,200 m²
With
Aire Homes

The brief

A brickworks made the bricks for half of Leeds, then stood empty for forty years. We were asked what it could make next.

The answer was homes, built from what was already there. We took down two unsafe sheds by hand, cleaned 310,000 bricks and laid them again as four blocks around the old drying yard.

A third of the homes are let at social rent and are impossible to tell from the rest. Every front door opens onto the yard, and the tall chimney still stands in the middle of it.

What it is made of

  • Reclaimed brick 310,000, cleaned on site
  • Timber floors Cross-laminated spruce
  • Lime mortar So the bricks can be used a third time
  • Zinc roofs Standing seam, 80-year life
  • Yard paving The old kiln floors, relaid
