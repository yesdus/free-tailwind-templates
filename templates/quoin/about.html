About

Why we exist

An architect who never has to live with the building learns very little from it. So we became the developer and the landlord too.

Quoin began in 2009 with two architects, a borrowed office and a terrace of four houses that no bank wanted to fund. We built them anyway, kept them, and still fix their gutters.

Sixteen years later the studio is thirty-one people: architects, site managers, a finance team of three and two caretakers who know more about how our buildings are used than anyone with a drawing board.

Three things we will not do

  1. 1 Sell and leave Every building stays on our books for at least ten years.
  2. 2 Hide the cheap homes Social rent shares the same stairs, bricks and views.
  3. 3 Knock down what stands If a frame can carry another life, it gets one.
