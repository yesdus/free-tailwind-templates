From field to front door

One team, five stages, no hand-offs

Most buildings pass through four companies before anyone moves in. Ours pass through one.

  1. 01 · Month 1

    Find

    We walk the site with the people who live beside it, before a line is drawn.

  2. 02 · Months 2 to 8

    Draw

    Sketch, model, test, repeat. Planning drawings go in when the neighbours have seen them.

  3. 03 · Months 6 to 10

    Fund

    We raise the money ourselves, which is why nobody can ask us to make it cheaper later.

  4. 04 · Months 11 to 30

    Build

    Our own site managers, the same four contractors for fifteen years.

  5. 05 · Year 3 onwards

    Keep

    We manage what we build. Every complaint comes back to the desk that drew the detail.
