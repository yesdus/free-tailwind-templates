Help center

Questions we hear a lot

How does the free plan work?

Basic is free for as long as you like. It includes fitness tracking and daily logs, and you can move to Pro whenever you need more.

Can I share my data with my doctor?

Yes. With Team Sync you choose who sees your health data and you can stop sharing at any time.

Which devices does Physiotech connect to?

The app works with the most common watches, bands and scales, and syncs in the background.

Is my health data safe?

Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and it is never sold to third parties.
