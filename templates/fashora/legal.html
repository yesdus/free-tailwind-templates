Privacy Policy

01

What we collect

Your name, contact details, delivery address and order history. Payment details are handled by our payment provider, never stored by us.

02

How we use it

To deliver your orders, answer your questions and, if you agree, tell you about new drops.

03

Your rights

Ask for a copy of your data, a correction or its deletion at any time by writing to privacy@fashora.example.

Terms of Service

01

Orders and prices

An order is confirmed when you receive our email. Prices include taxes and exclude delivery, shown at checkout.

02

Returns

You have thirty days to return unworn pieces for a full refund. Exchanges for another size are free.

03

Membership

Memberships renew monthly until cancelled and can be ended at any time from your account.

Cookie Policy

02

Optional cookies
