Made To Move

Streetwear built in small runs for people who live in their clothes, not in front of a mirror.

Talk to the crew

From One Jacket To a Whole Crew

Fashora started in 2019 with a single modular shell, cut in a shared studio and sold to friends. They wore it everywhere, so we kept going.

We still design every piece ourselves, test it on the street, and only make what we would wear every day.
