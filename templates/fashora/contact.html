Before You Ask

How long does shipping take?

Orders leave our warehouse within one working day. Delivery takes two to four days at home and five to nine days abroad.

Can I return or exchange a piece?

Yes. You have thirty days to send back anything unworn, and exchanges for another size are free.

How do I find my size?

Every product page has a measured size chart. If you are between sizes, write to us and we will help you choose.

When do new drops go live?

Members hear first by email. Drops open to everyone forty-eight hours later, while stock lasts.
