Technology

Three tools, one quiet goal: catch it early

01

The scan pod

Light, sound and gentle electrical signals map your heart, skin and body composition in twenty minutes. No needles, no X-rays.

  • Heart rhythm & HRV
  • Body composition
  • Full skin map

02

The wristband

Between visits it records sleep, heart rate and activity, and syncs once a day. Battery lasts nine days.

  • Sleep stages
  • Resting heart rate
  • Activity trends

03

The model

Trained on 2.1 million anonymised scans, it compares you with your own baseline and flags real changes, not noise.

  • Personal baselines
  • Explained flags
  • Doctor review on every alert
