Questions, answered

Can I freeze my membership?
Yes — up to eight weeks a year for travel, illness or life in general.
Is there a joining fee?
No. You pay your first month (or year) and that's it.
Can I switch plans?
Any time. Changes apply from your next billing date.
Do you offer a free trial?
Every new member gets two free weeks on the Transform plan.
What if I can't make it to a studio?
Online Coaching is included in Transform and Elite, so your plan travels with you.
Do you have student or senior rates?
Yes, 15% off any plan with a valid ID.
