Questions, answered

What counts as one request?

One page, one flow, one brand asset or a set of small fixes. If it's bigger, we split it into steps.

Can I pause my plan?

Yes. Pause any time and your unused days roll over for up to 12 months.

Do you write copy?

We shape messaging and headlines. Long-form copy is available as an add-on.

Which platforms do you build on?

Webflow, WordPress, Shopify or plain code. We'll recommend what fits your team.

Is there a contract?

No. Plans are month to month. Quarterly billing saves 10%.

Do you offer refunds?

If you're not happy after the first week, we refund 75% of the month.
