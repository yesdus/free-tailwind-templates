The problem

A template shop that was slow on phones, buried the subscription offer and lost a third of buyers at shipping.

What we did

New product pages with brew guides, a two-step checkout and a subscription toggle on every bag.

What changed

Orders tripled by spring, subscriptions became 40% of revenue, and support emails halved.
