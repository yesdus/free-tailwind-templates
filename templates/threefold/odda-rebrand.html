All work

Odda: fewer pieces, said more clearly.

Client
Odda, slow-fashion label
Strands
Strategy, craft, experience
Timeline
19 weeks, 2025 to 2026
Team
Five people, one table

01

The question

Odda made twelve garments a year and sold them as if it made two hundred. The founders asked for a new logo. What they needed was a reason for a customer to wait six weeks for a jacket.

02

Strategy

Forty interviews later the answer was already in their workshop: every piece is numbered and repaired for life. We built the position around ownership instead of newness, and rewrote the range into four permanent lines.

03

Craft

The identity borrows from the repair ledger. A numeral set drawn for stitching, a wordmark that sits in a garment label at eight millimetres, and a palette taken from undyed cloth.

04

Experience

The store shows what is being made this month instead of what is in stock. Each product page carries its repair history, and waiting customers get a photo from the cutting table.

“They talked us out of the logo we came in asking for and into a position we could actually defend.”
Ingrid Aasen, founder of Odda
