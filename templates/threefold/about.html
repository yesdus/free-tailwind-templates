Why we stayed small

Threefold began in 2017 when a strategist, a designer and a developer left three different agencies with the same complaint: the work was good until it was handed over.

So we take on eight projects a year, put a person from each strand on every one, and never pass a client to a team they have not met.

How a project runs

  1. Step 1

    Listen

    Two weeks of interviews with customers, staff and the people who said no to you.

  2. Step 2

    Decide

    One position, tested in the market, signed off before any design begins.

  3. Step 3

    Make

    Identity, language and the first real things built side by side.

  4. Step 4

    Hand over

    Guidelines, files and training, plus ninety days of support after launch.
