Ember Corner Café

A neighbourhood café in Porto where warm materials and smarter seating turned quick takeaways into long visits.

Type
Hospitality
Size
150 m²
Scope
Material Direction
Year
2026

Before

Bright, loud and hard: guests grabbed coffee and left, and the back half of the room sat empty most afternoons.

After

Softer lighting, rattan seating and a planted divider created quieter corners. Average visits are now 25 minutes longer.
