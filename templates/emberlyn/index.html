We exist to share the joy of a celebration. Every story we tell is shaped around yours: tender or playful, wild or quietly classic. We look after every small detail so you can stay inside the moments that matter.

Hi, I'm Elias

For ten years I worked in newsrooms, chasing deadlines across three countries. A friend's wedding in Porto changed the plan: I came home with four hundred photos and a new job. Today I photograph around thirty weddings a year, mostly couples who want honest pictures that still feel like them in thirty years.
