Good to know

How far ahead should we book?
Most couples book 12 to 18 months ahead. Weekdays and winter dates often open up later.
Do you travel?
Yes. Travel within Portugal is included; anywhere else is at cost, no markup.
When do we get our photos?
A preview within 72 hours, the full gallery within six weeks.
Can we choose the music in the film?
Absolutely. We license any track you love, or suggest a few.
Is a deposit required?
30% holds your date. The rest is due two weeks before the wedding.
What if it rains?
Then we get the best photos. We bring clear umbrellas just in case.
