Five things most people come in for

All treatments
  1. 01 Check-up and clean Forty minutes with a dentist and a hygienist, and photos of anything we find. from £45
  2. 02 White fillings Matched to the tooth, set in one visit, and guaranteed for five years. from £95
  3. 03 Whitening Custom trays made here and a gel you use at home for two weeks. from £290
