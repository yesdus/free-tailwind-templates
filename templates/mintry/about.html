The practice

Nine people, three chairs, no hurry

We opened in 2012 with one idea: a dental practice run the way a nervous patient would run it.

It started with a patient who walked out

In her last hospital job, Amara Bello watched a man leave halfway through a check-up because nobody had told him what was about to happen. She wrote down everything that would have kept him in the chair. That list became Mintry.

Appointments here are a third longer than usual. Every room has a screen above the chair so you see what we see. Prices are on the wall, on the website and on your plan before anything begins.

Four thousand patients later, the list is still pinned up in the staff room.
