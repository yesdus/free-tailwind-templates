Everything explained before we start

Four of the things we do most, in plain words. If yours is not here, it is probably on the fees page, and we are happy to talk it through.

Everyday

Check-up and clean

A dentist checks every tooth, your gums and the soft parts of your mouth. A hygienist then cleans and polishes. You leave with photos and a plan, even if the plan is to come back in a year.

  • X-rays only when you are due them
  • Photos of anything we find
  • A written plan with prices
It takes
40 minutes

Everyday

White fillings

We take out the decay, rebuild the tooth in a resin matched to its colour and polish it until your tongue cannot find the join. Old grey fillings can be swapped the same way.

  • Numb before we start, always
  • Guaranteed for five years
  • Eat as soon as the numbness fades
It takes
30 to 50 minutes
It costs
from £95

Cosmetic

Clear aligners

A scan instead of putty, a 3D preview of the result before you commit, then a new pair of thin clear trays every week. Most people around you will not notice them.

  • Free consultation and preview
  • Whitening included at the end
  • Retainers for life in the price
It takes
6 to 12 months
It costs
from £1,900

Restoring

Implants

A small titanium post replaces the root, and a crown made for the gap sits on top. It is planned on a scan, placed here under local anaesthetic and feels like a tooth.

  • One dentist from scan to crown
  • Sedation if you would like it
  • Ten-year guarantee on the implant
It takes
3 to 5 months
It costs
from £2,200

Your first visit

Forty minutes, and no surprises

  1. 1

    A chat, sitting upright

    We ask what you want from your teeth and what has put you off before. Nothing happens in the first ten minutes.

  2. 2

    A careful look

    Teeth, gums and jaw, with a camera so you can see what we see on the screen above the chair.

  3. 3

    A plan to take home

    What needs doing, what can wait and what each part costs. You decide in your own time.

Asked every week

Before you book

Will it hurt?
We numb the gum with a gel first and wait until it has worked. If you feel anything, raise a hand and we stop.
I have not been for years. Is that a problem?
No. About half of our new patients say the same. Nobody will tell you off.
Do you see children?
Yes, from their first tooth. Under twelves are seen free when a parent is a patient.
Can I spread the cost?
Anything over £300 can be paid over six or twelve months with no interest.
